The engineering implication is to place a release check at the property actually
required: numerical comparison for numerical behavior, the real getter for
output-access success, Task creation for that configured cardinality guard,
and the full artifact-linked map for qualified display agreement. Existing
protections remain useful within these scopes. The paper makes no claim that
developers generally lack such tests.
