## Working schedule if SQA4AI is selected

The [official 2027 CFP](https://sqa4ai-ws.github.io/2027/index.html) lists abstract
submission **19 October 2026** and paper submission **23 October 2026**, AoE
(UTC−12). Treat abstract entry as a required milestone; do not assume it can be
skipped. The four-page cap is conservatively treated as including references.

- Before the abstract milestone: decide venue/category and authors, verify the
  core thesis against human-inspected evidence, and settle whether the anonymous
  deposit can be delivered in time.
- Before the full-paper milestone: resolve retained report-only secondary claims,
  supply Data Availability/access, finish disclosure/anonymity/length checks, and
  obtain explicit author approval to submit.
- If a core claim remains unsupported or the evidence cannot be made adequately
  inspectable, narrow the paper or use a later venue. Do not relabel an unverified
  claim to meet the deadline.

These are a working plan, not promised future actions or an actual registration.
The CFP's camera-ready year is inconsistent with the parent conference page;
[VENUES.md](VENUES.md) records it rather than silently correcting the source.

## What does not block a bounded paper by itself

- Zero newly reproduced natural EfficientNet defects; injected controls answer
  a mechanism question, not prevalence.
- E5 adding zero rejection beyond matched existing checks; it stays auxiliary.
- No stable-synset/training lineage for P3, provided the claim remains qualified
  display-order disagreement rather than prediction correctness.
- No exhaustive external runtime sweep, universal metadata semantics or new
  general analyzer. Do not start these merely to inflate the paper.
- Missing later reconstruction records if the retained core claims have their
  own adequate original evidence and the manuscript accurately states the gap.

## Stop expansion rule

After core author verification, inspectable evidence, accurate claim scope and
venue compliance, freeze the paper for submission. Add an experiment only for a
specific unresolved question that can change a retained claim. Keep the existing
negative results, related-work overlap and same-device native-integration limit.

Agent work can prepare source, indexes, checks and an anonymous local derivative.
It cannot complete human audit, decide authorship, certify novelty/acceptance or
submit on behalf of authors without their explicit instruction.
